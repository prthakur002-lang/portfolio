Introduction

About Me

Hi, I'm Prem Rajusingh Thakur — a first-year Information Technology student at Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Akurdi. Born on 25 October 2006, I grew up at Sainiki Vidyalaya, Sagroli, Nanded — a school that instilled discipline, duty, and determination from a very young age.

Army was always my first dream. Growing up in a Sainiki school surrounded by the spirit of service and sacrifice, I always saw myself wearing the uniform of the Indian Army. The NCC shaped me deeply — I became a CDT with the 52 Maharashtra Battalion, completed the Annual Training Camp AIC-219, earned my NCC 'A' Certificate with Grade A, and won the 🥇 Gold Medal in Firing — a moment I will carry with pride forever. But life had a different path for me — my eyesight meant the direct army route wasn't possible. So I chose the next best battlefield: technology. Engineering is my new mission.

As a Maharashtra State-level competitive swimmer, I represented at the राज्यस्तरीय शालेय जलतरण क्रीडा स्पर्धा 2022–23, competing in the 4×100m Freestyle event at Balewadi, Pune. Swimming taught me that the race is won in the mind before it's won in the water.

Outside academics and sport, I love horse riding and carry the adventurous spirit of a true Sainiki school student.
